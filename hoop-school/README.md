# Hoop School 3D

An animated 3D basketball tutorial for kids that runs in the browser: 31 lessons covering basics, tricks, rules, team plays and pro tactics, with slow motion, step-by-step captions and a free camera.

Open `index.html` (served by GitHub Pages at `/hoop-school/`).

## Credits

- 3D rendering: [three.js](https://threejs.org) r128, loaded from cdnjs and jsDelivr.
- Player and referee model, skeleton and walk/run/idle animations: the "Xbot" character from the three.js examples (originally from Adobe Mixamo).
- Fonts: Lilita One and Nunito from Google Fonts.
